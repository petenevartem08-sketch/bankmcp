## 🏦 What Is BankMCP?

BankMCP is a free, self-hosted tool that connects your AI assistant (like Claude, ChatGPT, or Ollama) directly to your bank account information. It works through secure open banking technology (PSD2 compliant) provided by Enable Banking. 

Think of it as a secure bridge. Your AI can ask questions about your transactions, balances, and spending patterns — and get real answers from your actual bank data. No more guessing or manual spreadsheet tracking.

**The best part?** It's completely read-only. BankMCP can look at your transactions but cannot move, send, or spend any money. Your funds remain 100% safe.

## 🔒 Is It Safe?

Yes. Here's why:

- **Read-Only Access:** BankMCP has permission to view your account data, nothing more. It cannot initiate transfers, pay bills, or change account settings.
- **Self-Hosted:** You run it on your own computer. Your financial data never goes through third-party servers.
- **Open Banking Standard:** Uses regulated PSD2 APIs through Enable Banking, the same secure infrastructure used by major financial institutions across Europe.
- **Local Processing:** All data stays on your machine unless you explicitly share it with your AI tool.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Will BankMCP work with my bank?**
If your bank supports PSD2 open banking (which most European banks do), BankMCP will work. The Enable Banking platform supports over 3,000 banks across Europe.

**Do I need programming skills?**
No. The setup wizard handles everything. If you can follow a recipe, you can set up BankMCP.

**Can my AI steal my money?**
Absolutely not. BankMCP operates in read-only mode. The AI can only view data, never modify it.

**Does this work with ChatGPT?**
Yes! BankMCP uses the standard MCP (Model Context Protocol), which is supported by ChatGPT, Claude, Ollama, and many other modern AI tools.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. BankMCP runs locally on your computer. Your bank data is only shared with the AI assistant you choose to connect.

**Is this legal?**
Yes. BankMCP uses official PSD2 open banking APIs with your explicit consent. It's the same technology used by major fintech apps like Revolut and Apple Pay.

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "I can't connect to my bank"
- Make sure you've completed the Enable Banking registration and verified your email
- Check that your bank is listed in Enable Banking's supported banks
- Try refreshing the connection after 5 minutes

### "My AI isn't responding to BankMCP"
- Verify the connection string is correctly pasted in your AI tool
- Restart both BankMCP and your AI application
- Check that you're using the latest version of BankMCP

### "The app closes immediately after launching"
- Make sure you're running Windows 10 or newer
- Try right-clicking the downloaded file and selecting "Run as administrator"
- Temporarily disable antivirus software and try again (if it works, add BankMCP to your antivirus whitelist)
